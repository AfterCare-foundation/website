Motion is already reduced

Your device is set to reduce motion (less sliding, fading and moving), so this page already is. We follow that setting, so the switch above stays on.

To change it: on iPhone, open Settings › Accessibility › Motion. On Android, open Settings › Accessibility and look for “Remove animations”.
