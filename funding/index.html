How AfterCare is funded

Two funding models were never on the table: advertising, and selling data. A sexual-health app that watches its users is one nobody should trust — including you.

So AfterCare is funded the boring way instead: grants, sponsorship, and partnerships with people who work in this field. What we're applying for gets listed here as it's confirmed.
